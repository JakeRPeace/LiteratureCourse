## 📜 Course Overview
Explore the decaying landscapes and fractured psyches of the American South. This mini-course covers five essential works that define the Southern Gothic genre through their exploration of the grotesque, the tragic, and the mythic.

### 📚 Reading List
1. **As I Lay Dying** — William Faulkner ⚪ *Not Started*
2. **Wise Blood** — Flannery O'Connor ⚪ *Not Started*
3. **The Heart Is a Lonely Hunter** — Carson McCullers ⚪ *Not Started*
4. **Outer Dark** — Cormac McCarthy ⚪ *Not Started*
5. **Other Voices, Other Rooms** — Truman Capote ⚪ *Not Started*
