# Study Guide Structure

Each book in your reading list follows a **three-part study guide system** designed to support you before, during, and after reading.

---

## 📚 The Three Documents

### 01 Pre-Reading Guide.md
**Read this BEFORE you start the book**

Contains:
- What the book is about (brief overview)
- Why it's important (literary significance, cultural impact, historical context)
- What to think about as you read (key questions, themes to watch for, literary elements to notice)
- Reading tips and goals

**Purpose:** Gives you context and focus before you begin reading.

---

### 02 Chapter-by-Chapter Notes.md
**Use this WHILE you read**

Contains:
- Section-by-section breakdown of the book
- Important plot points for each chapter/section
- Key themes, symbols, and literary techniques
- Questions to consider as you read
- Space for your own notes

**Purpose:** Guides you through the book, highlighting important points and helping you track your thoughts.

---

### 03 Post-Reading Analysis.md
**Read this AFTER you finish the book**

Contains:
- In-depth exploration of major themes
- Complete analysis of symbolism
- Literary techniques and style analysis
- Key quotes with detailed interpretation
- Critical interpretations
- Final reflection questions

**Purpose:** Deepens your understanding and helps you synthesize what you've read.

---

## 🎯 How to Use This System

### For Each Book:

1. **Before Reading:**
   - Read `01 Pre-Reading Guide.md`
   - Get context and know what to look for
   - Consider the questions posed

2. **While Reading:**
   - Read a chapter/section
   - Review the corresponding section in `02 Chapter-by-Chapter Notes.md`
   - Write your own notes in the "Your Notes" sections
   - Don't read ahead in the guide - let it guide you as you go

3. **After Reading:**
   - Read `03 Post-Reading Analysis.md`
   - Reflect on the deeper meanings
   - Answer the final reflection questions
   - Write your own final thoughts

---

## 📝 Templates for Future Books

When adding a new book, use the templates in the `11 Books/` folder:

- `_TEMPLATE 01 Pre-Reading Guide.md`
- `_TEMPLATE 02 Chapter-by-Chapter Notes.md`
- `_TEMPLATE 03 Post-Reading Analysis.md`

Copy each template into your new book's folder and fill in the book-specific information.

See `EXPANSION GUIDE.md` for detailed instructions on adding new books.

---

## ✅ Benefits of This Structure

- **Pre-Reading Guide:** Prevents you from missing important context or themes
- **Chapter Notes:** Helps you track important points without getting lost
- **Post-Reading Analysis:** Deepens understanding and helps you see the bigger picture

This three-part system ensures you get the most out of each book while maintaining a consistent study approach across all your readings.
