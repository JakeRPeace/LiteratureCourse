## 📜 Course Overview
This course focuses on major works of modern literature from the mid-1800s onward, chosen for their cultural importance, stylistic range, and readability.

### 📚 Reading List
1. **The Old Man and the Sea** — Ernest Hemingway
2. **The Stranger** — Albert Camus
3. **The Great Gatsby** — F. Scott Fitzgerald
4. **The Death of Ivan Ilyich** — Leo Tolstoy
5. **Jane Eyre** — Charlotte Brontë
6. **Mrs Dalloway** — Virginia Woolf
7. **Notes from Underground** — Fyodor Dostoevsky
8. **Things Fall Apart** — Chinua Achebe
9. **The Left Hand of Darkness** — Ursula K. Le Guin
10. **Beloved** — Toni Morrison
