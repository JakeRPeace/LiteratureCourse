## 📜 Course Overview
The art of life-writing. This course explores how the human experience is captured through autobiography and formal biography, from historical figures to modern reflections.

### 📚 Reading List
1. **The Autobiography of Malcolm X** — Malcolm X & Alex Haley
2. **I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings** — Maya Angelou
3. **Educated** — Tara Westover
4. **Meditations** — Marcus Aurelius
5. **The Diary of a Young Girl** — Anne Frank
6. **Team of Rivals: The Political Genius of Abraham Lincoln** — Doris Kearns Goodwin
7. **Born a Crime** — Trevor Noah
8. **The Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin** — Benjamin Franklin
9. **A Promised Land** — Barack Obama
10. **When Breath Becomes Air** — Paul Kalanithi
