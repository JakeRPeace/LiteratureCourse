# Great Literature 106 - Southern Gothic (Mini-Course)

*Southern Gothic: A study of the decaying, the grotesque, and the mythic darkness of the American South.*

---

## 🎯 Goals & Constraints

* Focus on the **Southern Gothic tradition** (mid-20th century).
* Explore themes of **decay, religion, racism, and social alienation**.
* Mini-course format: **5 essential works**.

---

## 📚 Core Reading List

1. *As I Lay Dying* — William Faulkner ⚪ *Not Started*
2. *Wise Blood* — Flannery O'Connor ⚪ *Not Started*
3. *The Heart Is a Lonely Hunter* — Carson McCullers ⚪ *Not Started*
4. *Outer Dark* — Cormac McCarthy ⚪ *Not Started*
5. *Other Voices, Other Rooms* — Truman Capote ⚪ *Not Started*

---

## 🗓️ Syllabus Structure

* 1 book every 2–3 weeks.
* Focus on the transition from traditional Gothic elements to the modern American grotesque.
