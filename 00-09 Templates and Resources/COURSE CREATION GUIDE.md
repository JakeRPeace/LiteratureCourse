# Course Creation Guide

This guide explains how to create a new literature course/syllabus in this collection.

---

## 🎯 Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Choose Your Area

Check `00 Master Index.md` to see available areas:
- 30-39: Available
- 40-49: Available
- 50-59: Available
- etc.

Each course gets its own 10-number area (e.g., 30-39, 40-49).

### 2. Create the Folder Structure

Create a folder named: `[Area] [Course Name]/`

**Example:** `30-39 Russian Literature/`

Inside, create these folders:
```
[Area] [Course Name]/
├── [Category 1] Primary Materials/ (e.g., "31 Books" or "31 Poetry")
├── [Category 2] Supplemental Readings/
├── [Category 3] Resources/
└── [Category 4] Notes and Reflections/
```

**Standard Categories:**
- **X1:** Primary materials (Books, Poetry, Short Stories, etc.)
- **X2:** Supplemental Readings
- **X3:** Resources
- **X4:** Notes and Reflections
- **X5-X9:** Reserved for course-specific needs

### 3. Create Index and README

Create two files in your course folder:

**`00 Index.md`**
- Lists all materials in the course
- Follow the format from existing courses
- Update as you add materials

**`README.md`**
- Quick start guide for the course
- Explains the course structure
- Points to templates and resources

### 4. Create Syllabus Document

Create a syllabus document at the root level:
`# [Course Name] – Living Syllabus.md`

Include:
- Course goals and constraints
- Reading list
- Themes/genres covered
- Structure (if planned)
- Reflection prompts

### 5. Add Your First Items

For each book/item:

1. **Create folder:** `[Category]X.XX [Title] - [Author]/`
2. **Copy templates** from `00-09 Templates and Resources/01 Study Guide Templates/`
3. **Rename templates** (remove `_TEMPLATE` prefix)
4. **Fill in** book-specific information
5. **Update** the course's `00 Index.md`

### 6. Update Master Index

Edit `00-09 Templates and Resources/00 Master Index.md`:
- Add your new course to the "Active Courses" section
- Mark the area as used
- Include basic course information

## 📋 Course Types and Adaptations

### For Poetry Courses

**Category Structure:**
- `31 Poetry Collections/` or `31 Individual Poems/`
- Adjust templates: "Chapter" → "Poem" or "Section"

**Syllabus Focus:**
- Poets and movements
- Poetic forms and devices
- Themes and imagery

### For Regional Literature (e.g., Russian Literature)

**Category Structure:**
- `31 Books/` (standard)
- May add `35 Historical Context/` for course-specific materials

**Syllabus Focus:**
- Historical/cultural context
- Translation considerations
- Regional themes and concerns
- Literary movements of the region

### For Genre Courses (e.g., Science Fiction)

**Category Structure:**
- `31 Books/` (standard)
- May add `35 Genre Resources/` for subgenre guides

**Syllabus Focus:**
- Genre conventions
- Subgenres and evolution
- Speculative elements
- Cultural impact

### For Short Story Collections

**Category Structure:**
- `31 Short Stories/` or `31 Collections/`
- May organize by author or collection

**Syllabus Focus:**
- Individual stories
- Collections as units
- Author styles
- Short story techniques

---

## 🎨 Customizing Templates

Templates are designed to be flexible. You can:

1. **Adjust section names** (Chapter → Poem → Story)
2. **Add course-specific sections** (e.g., "Translation Notes" for foreign literature)
3. **Modify questions** to fit your course focus
4. **Add genre-specific elements** (e.g., "World-building" for fantasy)

The templates are starting points - adapt them to your needs!

## 📚 Example: Creating "Russian Literature" Course

1. **Choose area:** 30-39
2. **Create:** `30-39 Russian Literature/`
3. **Create folders:**
   - `31 Books/`
   - `32 Supplemental Readings/`
   - `33 Resources/`
   - `34 Notes and Reflections/`
4. **Create files:**
   - `00 Index.md`
   - `README.md`
   - `# Russian Literature – Living Syllabus.md` (at root)
5. **Add first book:**
   - `31.01 Crime and Punishment - Fyodor Dostoevsky/`
   - Copy templates, fill in details
6. **Update:** `00-09 Templates and Resources/00 Master Index.md`

- **Adapt templates** as you discover what works
- **Document changes** in your course's notes section
